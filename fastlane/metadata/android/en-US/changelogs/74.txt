New
- A new look: softer shapes, grouped lists, and one base colour for the whole launcher. Pick one of eight colours, Black, or Chameleon, which follows the wallpaper (Settings > Appearance).
- The wallpaper scrolls with the home pages (can be turned off in Settings > Home screen).
- Private space (Android 15+): set one up in Android's settings and the app drawer gets a Private tab to unlock, use and lock it; search finds it; it can hide while locked.
