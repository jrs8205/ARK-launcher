New
- Screensaver: ARK-Launcher can be chosen as Android's screensaver. Six styles and a settings page of its own.
- Icon shapes: eight shapes for every app icon (Settings > Icons).
- A charging animation on the home screen and in the launcher's status bar.
- Home folders: add several apps from a list, sort A–Z.

Changed
- Two new optional permissions: notifications (battery-full alert) and wake lock (start on charge).
- The name is now written ARK-Launcher.
